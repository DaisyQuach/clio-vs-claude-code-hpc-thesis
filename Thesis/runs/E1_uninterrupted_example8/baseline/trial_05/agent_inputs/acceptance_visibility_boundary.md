# Acceptance and Visibility Boundary

## What you can see
Everything in `agent_inputs/` (this frozen configuration, the two Morphorm manuals) and your own trial workspace.

## What you cannot see and must not look for
- Reference meshes, decks, runs, histories, topologies, figures, numerical targets and tolerances.
- Scoring criteria beyond the deliverable list and locked settings in `scientific_problem_specification.md`.
- Other trials' workspaces, pilot runs, researcher records, and the Morphorm installation/source tree.

## How the trial is judged (what you need to know)
- The trial is scored after your process ends, by the researcher, against the frozen specification: locked formulation and settings
  as actually executed (generated configuration), completed iteration count, required deliverables with correct names, provenance record,
  and scientific agreement of the result with a researcher-held reference.
- A process that exits cleanly is not a successful trial. Missing deliverables, unverified settings, or a run that did not reach the
  required iteration count are not successes.
- Any attempt to read restricted material is detectable from the session record and disqualifies the trial.
